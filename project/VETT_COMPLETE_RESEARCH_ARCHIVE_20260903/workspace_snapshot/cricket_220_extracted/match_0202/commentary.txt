The issue for Eastport was that the innings never found a second partnership of the same weight. Eastport’s bowlers had meaningful moments: Owen Sinclair’s two wickets and tidy spell kept pressure in the chase, Mason Cole bowled ten disciplined overs, Dylan Foster struck first, and Ryan Brooks had Samuel Cross. Their problem was control between wickets. Twenty-five extras, including twenty-two wides, softened almost every squeeze they created. Ridgeway were not flawless, but they were more connected across the full match: Miles Turner and Harry Blake held Eastport below a bigger score, the top order gave the chase a platform, Marcus Flynn restored the tempo, and Callum Reed carried the match through every late complication. Ridgeway reached one hundred and eighty-three for six, won by four wickets, and qualified because they had the one batter who refused to let the chase lose its main thread.
