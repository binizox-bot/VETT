| L3 | Hidden innings-role inference | Requires connecting text description with table roles. | Callum Shaw is presented as the rescue-and-finish batter after Harborline fall to 84/6, not merely as a top scorer. |
| L3 | Pressure-state reasoning | Requires understanding why a score becomes match-winning. | Reef Bay have one middle stand to 72/3, then lose the chase through a long squeeze and Harvey Blake’s lower-order strikes. |
