# R2 Blind Query Shortcut Audit

The audit examined 36 value-free queries across three instances. It found an obvious shortcut for 29 queries, an overall shortcut rate of **80.6%**.

| Instance | Queries | Shortcuts | No shortcut | Shortcut rate |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| `match_0185.R2FS` | 12 | 11 | 1 | 91.7% |
| `match_0106.R2FS` | 12 | 10 | 2 | 83.3% |
| `match_0178.R2FS` | 12 | 8 | 4 | 66.7% |
| **Overall** | **36** | **29** | **7** | **80.6%** |

The most common shortcut is a simple local calculation from adjacent partnership boundaries, batter shares, or extras components. Bowling-wicket queries are also frequently exposed by compact end-of-passage victim ledgers. A smaller set can be solved by applying a single stated decrement to a provisional display.

The seven queries marked as having no shortcut are batter or bowling rows that require resolving event identity or accumulating corrected contributions across multiple separated passages. No target values are included in the audit.
