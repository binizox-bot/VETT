| L1 | Direct extraction | Basic match facts are directly visible from the table and text. | Capeview won by 117 runs; Ethan Vale was Player of the Match; Capeview made 204 all out and Meridian made 87 all out. |
| L1 | Key player identification | Main performers can be found without calculation. | Ethan Vale scored 52 and took two early wickets; Noah Cole made 39; Owen Barrett made 34; Connor Wells took three wickets. |
| L2 | Cricket-rule arithmetic | Requires checking batting runs, extras, final totals, and result margin. | Capeview batting runs add to 191 and extras add 13, giving 204; Meridian batting runs add to 78 and extras add 9, giving 87; 204 minus 87 gives the 117-run margin. |
| L2 | Partnership and FOW consistency | Requires cumulative verification across partnerships and fall-of-wickets. | Capeview partnerships move through 68, 85, 93, 110, 145, 177, 179, 184, 189, and 204; Meridian partnerships move through 12, 12, 20, 31, 36, 45, 47, 74, 82, and 87. |
| L2 | Bowling reconciliation | Requires understanding that byes and leg-byes are not charged to bowlers. | Meridian bowlers concede 203 bowler-charged runs while Capeview total 204 because one run is a leg-bye; Capeview bowlers concede 85 charged runs while Meridian total 87 because two runs are byes/leg-byes. |
| L3 | Commentary-hidden inference | Important facts are embedded through match flow rather than dense score strings. | The text describes a strong opening stand, a middle-overs wobble, Ethan Vale’s repair innings, and Meridian’s chase collapsing in phases. |
| L3 | Narrative-to-table validation | The reader must connect commentary phrases to table evidence. | “The opening pair had already done the heavy first job,” “Ethan rebuilt the innings from the awkward middle,” and “the only late resistance came through Isaac Rowe and Daniel Reed” map to the partnership and FOW tables. |
