| L1 | Direct extraction | Basic match facts are visible directly from the table and commentary. | Ridgefield A won by 51 runs; Adrian Stone was Player of the Match; Ridgefield made 253/8 and Coastline were all out for 202. |
| L1 | Key player identification | Main contributors can be identified without calculation. | Adrian Stone made 76; Caleb Hayes made 41; Ryan Foster finished not out on 30; Zane Perry and Lucas Reed each took three wickets. |
| L2 | Cricket-rule arithmetic | Requires checking batting runs, extras, final totals, and victory margin. | Ridgefield batting runs add to 242 and extras add 11, giving 253; Coastline batting runs add to 183 and extras add 19, giving 202; 253 minus 202 gives the 51-run margin. |
| L2 | Partnership and FOW consistency | Requires cumulative verification across partnership and fall-of-wickets tables. | Ridgefield partnerships move through 86, 134, 146, 177, 215, 244, 248, 252, and 253; Coastline partnerships move through 8, 29, 62, 96, 139, 147, 178, 184, 189, and 202. |
| L2 | Bowling reconciliation | Requires understanding that byes and leg-byes are not charged to bowlers. | Coastline bowlers concede 250 charged runs while Ridgefield total 253 because three runs are byes/leg-byes; Ridgefield bowlers concede 201 charged runs while Coastline total 202 because one run is a leg-bye. |
| L2 | Ball-count consistency | Requires checking legal balls against innings length. | Ridgefield batters face 288 legal balls across 48.0 overs; Coastline batters face 260 legal balls across 43.2 overs. |
| L3 | Commentary-hidden inference | Key facts are embedded through rhythm and match pressure rather than dense score listing. | The text presents Adrian Stone as the innings architect, Ryan Foster and Dylan Price as late accelerators, and Coastline’s chase as repeated bursts cut off before becoming decisive. |
| L3 | Narrative-to-table validation | Readers must connect descriptive phrases to scorecard evidence. | “The opening stand gave Ridgefield their first command,” “Daniel and Luke kept Coastline alive for a while,” and “Zane and Lucas closed the chase in phases” map to partnership, FOW, and bowling tables. |
