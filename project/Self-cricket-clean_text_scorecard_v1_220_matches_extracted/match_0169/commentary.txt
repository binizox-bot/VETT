It was not a wild start in the style of pure hitting. It was more damaging than that because it lasted. What made the stand especially costly for Sandridge was the mixture of proper batting and unnecessary gifts. The partnership reached 141, and 18 of those came through extras, which meant Bayfront were getting score movement even when the batters were not forcing the ball away. That is the kind of early waste that hurts badly in age-group fifty-over cricket, because it lets a batting side settle without having to take on extra risk. Nolan Hayes, Carter Quinn, Gavin Ford, Parker Reed, and even Adrian Wells all gave away enough width or looseness at different moments to prevent any sustained squeeze from forming.
