| L2 | Partnership and FOW consistency | Requires cumulative verification across partnerships and fall-of-wickets. | Moorland partnerships move through 1, 27, 40, 48, 51, 109, 160, 169, 173, and 181; Southport partnerships move through 132, 151, 164, and 184. |
| L2 | Bowling reconciliation | Requires understanding that leg-byes are not charged to bowlers. | Southport bowlers concede 179 charged runs while Moorland total 181 because two runs are leg-byes; Moorland bowlers concede 183 charged runs while Southport total 184 because one run is a leg-bye. |
| L2 | Ball-count consistency | Requires checking legal balls against innings length. | Moorland batters face 257 legal balls across 42.5 overs; Southport batters face 182 legal balls across 30.2 overs. |
| L3 | Commentary-hidden inference | Key facts are embedded through rhythm, pressure, and match turning points rather than dense scoreboard listing. | The text presents Moorland’s early collapse, Dominic-and-Hayden’s repair, and Southport’s opening chase as the decisive hidden structure. |
| L3 | Narrative-to-table validation | Readers must connect descriptive match flow to scorecard evidence. | “Five down before the innings had shape,” “Dominic first held and then pushed,” and “Liam turned the chase from a target into a formality” map to FOW, batting, and partnership evidence. |
