Greenfield Academy Ground, Midlands. A low-scoring affair on a surface that gave the bowlers enough encouragement to make every run feel like a minor victory. Southmere won the toss and chose to bat, a decision that would be tested almost immediately as the ball began to move around under overcast skies. The innings never got going. Rowan Miles fell for 2 at 5 for 1 to Harvey Wells, the early breakthrough that set an anxious tone. Caleb Turner was next, gone for 9 to Ethan Vale at 17 for 2 — with the benefit of hindsight, the kind of dismissal that told you this was going to be a bowlers' day. Elliot Mason went for 4 to Finley Brooks at 21 for 3, and when Felix Clarke followed for 3 to Gavin Stone at 23 for 4, Southmere were staring at the kind of collapse from which recoveries are rarely mounted. Riley Vance made 5 and Parker Lane contributed 8, their partnership of 9 for the seventh wicket at least pushing the score from 44 for 6 to 53 for 7. Sawyer Grant chipped in with 13 and Parker Lane added 8 as the innings scraped to 81 all out from 30.5 overs — all ten wickets gone. Gavin Stone was the chief destroyer with 4 for 23, Ethan Vale supported with 3 for 15, the two of them accounting for seven of the ten dismissals in a brutally efficient display of seam bowling. The extras column read 17 — 14 wides spraying about as the bowlers occasionally lost their radar, a bye and two leg-byes — with the batting contributing just 64 and the bowling charge at 78.

Chasing 82 to win, Eastford's openers knew that the target was modest but the surface had already claimed ten wickets and was showing no signs of settling. Dominic Shaw took ownership of the chase with 30 from 24 deliveries — 3 fours and a six — an innings that was equal parts patience and power, the kind of knock around which small chases are built. Eastford reached 82 for 3 from just 14.3 overs, winning by 7 wickets. The chase extras were 16 — 14 wides and a no-ball and a leg-bye — with the batting producing 66 and the bowling charge at 81. Theo Reed took 1 for 9 and Wyatt Brooks 1 for 18 for Southmere, but the total was simply too small to defend. Gavin Stone was named Player of the Match for his 4 for 23, a spell that had effectively decided the contest before the chase had even begun. Greenfield Academy Ground had witnessed a match where the ball dominated the bat, and one bowler dominated both.
