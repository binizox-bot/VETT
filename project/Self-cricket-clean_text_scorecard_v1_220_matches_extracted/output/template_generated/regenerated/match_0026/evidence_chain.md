| L1 | Direct extraction | Basic match facts remain clearly obtainable from the table and commentary. | Ridgeport won by 218 runs; Nathan Wells was Player of the Match; Ridgeport made 251/4 and Coral Bay made 33 all out. |
| L1 | Key player identification | The main batting and bowling figures can be directly located without multi-step inference. | Nathan Wells scored 116; Leo Marsh made 61; Dylan Cole took five wickets; Ben Carter took two early wickets. |
| L2 | Cricket-rule arithmetic | The score requires checking batting runs, extras, final total, and victory margin. | Ridgeport batting runs add to 227 and extras add 24, giving 251; Coral Bay batting runs add to 20 and extras add 13, giving 33; 251 minus 33 gives the 218-run margin. |
| L2 | Partnership and FOW consistency | The reader must verify that partnership totals accumulate correctly into the fall-of-wickets chain. | Ridgeport partnerships move through 29, 179, 230, 242, and 251; Coral Bay partnerships move through 11, 13, 19, 21, 21, 21, 25, 25, 27, and 33. |
| L2 | Bowling reconciliation | The table requires understanding that byes and leg byes are not charged to bowlers. | Coral Bay bowlers concede 249 bowler-charged runs while Ridgeport total 251 because two runs are byes/leg-byes; Ridgeport bowlers concede all 33 because Coral Bay had no byes or leg-byes. |
| L3 | Commentary-hidden inference | Key facts are embedded through match rhythm rather than repeated as a scoreboard chain. | The text describes a major second-wicket stand, a late acceleration, a strangled chase, and a middle-order collapse without mechanically listing every FOW. |
| L3 | Narrative-to-table validation | The reader must connect phrasing to table evidence. | “A stand that carried the innings past the safe zone,” “a chase moving mostly through extras,” and “Dylan Cole closing the tail” all map to specific table events. |
