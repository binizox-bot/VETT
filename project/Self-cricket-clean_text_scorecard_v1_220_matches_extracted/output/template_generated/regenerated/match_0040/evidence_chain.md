| L1 | Direct extraction | Basic match facts can be read directly from the table and commentary. | Ridgeford A won by 151 runs; Elliot Vale was Player of the Match; Ridgeford made 293/9 and Coastbay were all out for 142. |
| L1 | Key player identification | Main contributors are identifiable without calculation. | Adrian Stone made 61, Brandon Hale made 58, Lucas Barrett made 52, and Elliot Vale took five wickets. |
| L2 | Cricket-rule arithmetic | Requires checking batting runs, extras, innings totals, and victory margin. | Ridgeford batting runs add to 276 and extras add 17, giving 293; Coastbay batting runs add to 131 and extras add 11, giving 142; 293 minus 142 gives the 151-run margin. |
| L2 | Partnership and FOW consistency | Requires cumulative verification across partnership and fall-of-wickets tables. | Ridgeford partnerships move through 92, 135, 154, 226, 251, 263, 274, 284, 291, and 293; Coastbay partnerships move through 61, 79, 85, 93, 109, 117, 127, 131, 138, and 142. |
| L2 | Bowling reconciliation | Requires understanding that byes and leg-byes are not charged to bowlers. | Coastbay bowlers concede 290 charged runs while Ridgeford total 293 because three runs are byes/leg-byes; Ridgeford bowlers concede 139 charged runs while Coastbay total 142 because three runs are byes/leg-byes. |
| L2 | Ball-count consistency | Requires checking legal balls against innings length. | Ridgeford batters face 300 legal balls across 50.0 overs; Coastbay batters face 196 legal balls across 32.4 overs. |
| L3 | Commentary-hidden inference | Key facts are embedded through rhythm and pressure rather than dense scoreboard listing. | The text presents Ridgeford’s layered batting, Coastbay’s promising opening stand, and Elliot Vale’s spell as the decisive hidden pivot. |
| L3 | Narrative-to-table validation | Readers must connect descriptive phrases to table evidence. | “The opening pair gave Ridgeford command,” “Lucas and Finley rebuilt the middle surge,” and “Elliot kept striking just as Coastbay tried to reset” map to score, partnership, and bowling fields. |
