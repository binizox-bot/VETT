| L1 | Direct extraction | Basic match facts can be read directly from the table and commentary. | Bayfront won by 94 runs; Tyler Stone was Player of the Match; Bayfront made 220/8 and Reefside were all out for 126. |
| L1 | Key player identification | Main contributors remain directly identifiable without calculation. | Tyler Stone scored 64; Connor Blake made 42; Leo Marsh made 39; Daniel Cross and Zane Cooper each took three wickets. |
| L2 | Cricket-rule arithmetic | Requires checking batting totals, extras, final totals, and victory margin. | Bayfront batting runs add to 203 and extras add 17, giving 220; Reefside batting runs add to 107 and extras add 19, giving 126; 220 minus 126 gives the 94-run margin. |
| L2 | Partnership and FOW consistency | Requires cumulative verification across partnerships and fall-of-wickets. | Bayfront partnerships move through 24, 84, 152, 161, 174, 206, 209, 215, and 220; Reefside partnerships move through 22, 47, 57, 58, 94, 120, 120, 123, 124, and 126. |
| L2 | Bowling reconciliation | Requires understanding that byes and leg-byes are not charged to bowlers. | Reefside bowlers concede 215 bowler-charged runs while Bayfront total 220 because five runs are byes/leg-byes; Bayfront bowlers concede 119 charged runs while Reefside total 126 because seven runs are byes/leg-byes. |
| L3 | Commentary-hidden inference | Key facts are embedded through rhythm, pressure, and turning points rather than dense scoreboard strings. | The text describes Tyler Stone’s early control, the Connor-Leo middle stand, Reefside’s slow chase, and the late collapse without listing every score node mechanically. |
| L3 | Narrative-to-table validation | The reader must connect descriptive phrases to specific table evidence. | “The central stand carried Bayfront into control,” “Mason’s stay consumed the chase,” and “Zane Cooper closed the tail” map back to partnership, batting, and bowling tables. |
