| L1 | Direct extraction | Basic match facts can be read directly from the table and commentary. | Northbridge won by 10 runs; Carter Vale was Player of the Match; Northbridge made 247/8 and Seacliff were all out for 237. |
| L1 | Key player identification | Major contributors remain visible without calculation. | Adrian Stone made 76; Carter Vale made 116; Jordan Pike took four wickets; Finley Lane finished not out on 35. |
| L2 | Cricket-rule arithmetic | Requires checking batting runs, extras, innings totals, and victory margin. | Northbridge batting runs add to 231 and extras add 16, giving 247; Seacliff batting runs add to 212 and extras add 25, giving 237; 247 minus 237 gives the 10-run margin. |
| L2 | Partnership and FOW consistency | Requires cumulative verification across partnerships and fall-of-wickets. | Northbridge partnerships move through 7, 103, 150, 168, 180, 219, 226, 237, and 247; Seacliff partnerships move through 0, 13, 13, 33, 67, 101, 107, 209, 229, and 237. |
| L2 | Bowling reconciliation | Requires understanding that byes and leg-byes are not charged to bowlers. | Seacliff bowlers concede 244 charged runs while Northbridge total 247 because three runs are byes/leg-byes; Northbridge bowlers concede 235 charged runs while Seacliff total 237 because two runs are leg-byes. |
| L2 | Ball-count consistency | Requires checking legal balls against innings length. | Northbridge batters face 288 legal balls across 50.0 overs with one no-ball and twelve wides outside legal delivery count; Seacliff batters face 250 legal balls across 41.4 overs. |
| L3 | Commentary-hidden inference | Key facts are embedded through match rhythm instead of dense score strings. | The text presents Northbridge’s phased total, Seacliff’s top-order collapse, Carter Vale’s rescue century, and the late collapse after the eighth-wicket stand as inferable pivots. |
| L3 | Narrative-to-table validation | Readers must connect descriptive phrases to table evidence. | “Adrian and Caleb rebuilt after the early wicket,” “Carter carried a broken chase,” and “the ninth-wicket break ended Seacliff’s last real route” map to batting, partnership, and FOW evidence. |
