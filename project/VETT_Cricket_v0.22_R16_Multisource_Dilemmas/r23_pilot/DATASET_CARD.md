# VETT-Cricket R23 Knowledge and Open-Reasoning Extension

R23 preserves R22 Tracks A and B byte-for-structure through private content digests, then adds:

- Track C: five closed-book cricket-law memory questions per match.
- Track D: five open explanations with deterministically scored conclusions, reasoning atoms and evidence graphs per match.

Rules are versioned to **Laws of Cricket 2017 Code, 4th Edition 2026**, effective **2026-10-01**. Official citations remain private during closed-book evaluation and are released with the gold package for audit.

Free-form explanations are retained for human or optional semantic review, but the primary score does not require an LLM judge.
